## What is NepTune?

As we know prolly that NepTune is the 8th positioned planet in our solar system. However, it arguebly the most COOLEST planet too. 
Jokes aside, NepTune is a intelligence layer (2nd Brain) where the data is stored in collections (or clusters). These collections are shareable, versionable, and recallable; enabling a community for the adoption of various knowledge nodes, AI (or Non-AI) apps to get context, and even automating the tasks.

## Why it exists

Nowadays, There are a lot of github repos (You can check https://github.com/trending for clarifications) that provides AI Skills, Agent.md, Prompts for the AI Agents to engineer them to perform certain works according to the way we want. 

We find this pretty frustrating to keep with the pace of the community. Every day new skill arrives and the previous gets improved, replaced, or obsolete, and We end up repeatedly research, download, configure and update them over and over again. 

Also, If We entered our needs to one AI agent and switch to other AI agent, We need to either `ctrl + c` the whole thing over and over again or We need to type that manually. 

Then, We asked
What if we could have a intelligence layer that makes other automatically gets synchronized?
